This Schedule governs **Faraday Academy**, Faraday's course and certification
programme, delivered through a third-party learning platform.

**The Faraday Intelligence Master Terms of Service are incorporated into this
Schedule by reference and apply in full.** They are published at
**https://faraday-intelligence.ai/terms**. This Schedule adds terms specific to
Faraday Academy. Where this Schedule conflicts with the Master Terms, this
Schedule controls for Faraday Academy only, and only to the extent of the
conflict. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Master
Terms.

## FA-1. Delivery on a third-party platform

Faraday Academy is delivered through **LearnWorlds**, a third-party learning
management system. Your use of that platform is also subject to the platform
provider's own terms and privacy policy, which you accept when you use it.

Faraday does not control the platform's availability, account handling, or data
practices. Course **content** is Faraday content under Section 4 of the Master
Terms; the **platform** is not. Where the platform's terms conflict with this
Schedule as to platform operation, the platform's terms govern the platform, and
this Schedule governs the content and the certificate.

## FA-2. Course access

Enrolment grants a named individual access to the enrolled course for the access
period stated at enrolment. **Section 2 (named-seat) of the Master Terms applies**:
course access, materials, and any downloadable workbook are for you alone and may
not be shared, resold, or circulated inside your organisation. Organisational and
cohort licensing is available separately by written agreement.

Course materials — video, slides, workbooks, assessments, and answer keys — may
not be recorded, redistributed, or reposted. **Section 5 (automated access,
scraping, and machine-learning use) applies in full**, including the prohibition on
using course material to train, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark, or ground any
model.

Faraday may update, reorganise, retire, or replace a course. Where a course you
are enrolled in is retired during your access period, we will provide either
continued access for the remainder of that period or a substitute course of
comparable scope.

## FA-3. Pricing and billing

Academy runs on a **pricing model separate from the Faraday subscription tiers**.
Course prices, bundles, cohort fees, and any subscription-style Academy plan are
published at the point of purchase.

Where an Academy purchase is a one-time course or bundle purchase, it does not
auto-renew. Where an Academy offering is sold as a recurring plan, the **Automatic
Renewal notice in the Master Terms applies to it in full**, including the
in-account cancel path, the cancellation email address, and the refund rule
(annual: pro-rata refund of the unused portion; monthly: no refund).

## FA-5. Certification

A Faraday Academy certificate **attests completion of the named course** — that
the enrolled individual completed the course requirements as assessed on the
platform, on the stated date, for the stated course version.

**A Faraday certificate is not an accredited professional credential.** Expressly,
it is not:

- a licence, registration, or certification issued or recognised by any
  government, regulator, or licensing board;
- accredited by any accrediting body, and it carries no continuing-education,
  CPD, CPE, PDH, or CEU credit unless a specific course states otherwise in
  writing;
- a professional qualification in engineering, law, finance, energy, or any other
  regulated field, and it does not authorise you to practise in any of them; and
- a warranty by Faraday of any holder's competence, judgement, or fitness for any
  role.

You may state that you completed the named Faraday Academy course and display the
certificate. You may **not** describe yourself as "Faraday certified" without
naming the course, describe the certificate as accredited or as a licence, alter a
certificate, or use it to imply Faraday endorses your work, your employer, or your
professional services. **Section 6 (no implied endorsement, no distortion) of the
Master Terms applies.**

Faraday may revoke a certificate obtained by fraud, assessment abuse, or account
sharing.

## FA-6. Token grants on completion

Where a course or certification grants **Faraday tokens** on completion:

- Tokens granted are governed by **Schedule BL (Briefing Library), BL-2 through
  BL-4**, which apply to tokens from every source. Tokens are a limited licence to
  access content, are not stored value, are not redeemable for cash, and are
  non-transferable.
- The grant is a promotional benefit of the course, not a separate purchase, and
  no part of the course price is allocated to it.

## FA-7. Nothing here is advice

Course content covers infrastructure, energy, permitting, and market topics for
**educational purposes only**. **Section 7 (No advice and no reliance), Section 8
(Scores, forecasts, and vintages), Section 9 (Third-party data pass-through), and
Section 10 (AI-assisted content) of the Master Terms apply in full.** Nothing
taught in Faraday Academy is investment, legal, engineering, siting, or permitting
advice, and completing a course does not qualify you to give any.

## FA-8. Accessibility

Faraday targets **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** for its own surfaces (Master Terms,
Section 17). Faraday Academy is delivered on a third-party platform whose
conformance Faraday does not control. If you encounter an accessibility barrier in
Academy content — including a need for captions, transcripts, or an alternative
format — email **accessibility@faraday-intelligence.ai** with the course and
lesson and we will work with you and, where the barrier is the platform's, with
the platform provider.
