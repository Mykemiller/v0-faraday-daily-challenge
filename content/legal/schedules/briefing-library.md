This Schedule governs the **Faraday Briefing Library** and the **Faraday tokens**
used to access content across Faraday products.

**The Faraday Intelligence Master Terms of Service are incorporated into this
Schedule by reference and apply in full.** This Schedule adds terms specific to the
Briefing Library and to tokens. Where this Schedule conflicts with the Master
Terms, this Schedule controls for the Briefing Library and for token matters only,
and only to the extent of the conflict. Capitalised terms not defined here have the
meaning given in the Master Terms.

## BL-1. What the Briefing Library is

The Briefing Library is a catalogue of Faraday briefings — theater, sector,
thread, and key-player analyses, jurisdiction reads, and related research
artefacts. Briefings are Faraday content under Section 4 of the Master Terms and
are produced through the pipeline disclosed in Section 10 (AI-assisted content).

Briefings carry a **vintage**. A briefing states Faraday's read as of its
publication date and is not updated in place unless it is republished with a new
vintage. Section 8 of the Master Terms applies to every score, forecast, and
posture referenced in a briefing.

## BL-2. Faraday tokens — what they are

**A Faraday token is a limited, revocable licence to access a unit of Faraday
content or functionality. It is not money.**

Tokens are, expressly:

- **not stored value**, not a deposit, not a bank or payment instrument, and not a
  balance held for you;
- **not a gift card, gift certificate, prepaid access instrument, or general-use
  prepaid card**;
- **not redeemable** for cash, credit, refund, or property, in whole or in part,
  by you or by anyone else;
- **not transferable, assignable, sellable, tradeable, or giftable**, and not
  usable by anyone other than the named individual on the account that holds them;
  and
- **not property**, and confer no ownership interest in Faraday or in any Faraday
  content.

Tokens are consumed when you unlock or retrieve gated content. A consumed token is
gone; unlocking the same item again may cost another token. Unlock durations,
token costs per action, and which actions are metered are set by Faraday and may
change prospectively.

Tokens may be **obtained** by purchase (in packs), **granted** with a subscription
plan, or **earned** as a promotional reward in another Faraday product (see
Schedule DC, DC-5). Tokens from every source are governed by this Schedule.

## BL-3. Purchased token packs

Token packs are **one-time purchases**, not subscriptions. They do not
auto-renew. Pack contents and prices are shown at the point of purchase and the
price charged is set by Faraday's server, never by the client.

Purchased tokens are credited to your account on successful payment. Because a
token is a licence to access content rather than a good, **token purchases are
final and non-refundable** except where a refund is required by law or where
Faraday determines a billing error or failed grant occurred. If a payment is
charged and the corresponding tokens are not credited, contact us and we will
credit them or refund the charge.

Plan-granted token allowances are part of your subscription, reset on the
subscription cycle, and do not carry over between cycles unless the plan says so.
Cancelling a subscription ends plan-granted allowances at the end of the paid
term; it does not, by itself, remove separately purchased tokens.

> **Current state of the product.** Faraday tokens are, as implemented today,
> **non-expiring** for separately purchased and earned balances. No expiry date,
> dormancy rule, service fee, or forfeiture mechanism exists in the product.
>
> **Why that is a legal question and not a product preference.** A non-expiring,
> prepaid, customer-funded balance can be characterised as stored value. If it is
> so characterised, several bodies of law may attach, including:
>
> - **State gift-card and prepaid-access statutes**, which variously restrict or
>   prohibit expiry dates and dormancy fees, mandate minimum validity periods, and
>   require specific pre-purchase disclosure;
> - **Federal prepaid-access and gift-certificate rules**, to the extent a token
>   pack is treated as a certificate rather than a licence;
> - **State unclaimed-property and escheat law**, under which an unredeemed
>   balance can become reportable and remittable to a state after a dormancy
>   period — an obligation that does not disappear because the issuer calls the
>   balance a "licence"; and
> - **Revenue-recognition treatment** of unredeemed balances (breakage), which
>   depends on the same characterisation.
>
> Whether Faraday's "licence to access content, consumed on use" framing (BL-2)
> holds up against these regimes is **exactly the question for counsel**. The
> framing is a good-faith description of the mechanism; it is not a determination.

> **What must NOT happen in the meantime.** Do not publish an invented expiry

## BL-5. Access, not ownership

Unlocking a briefing gives you access to read it on your named seat for the
period stated at unlock. It does not transfer ownership, does not grant a right to
redistribute, and does not survive termination of your account.

**Section 5 (automated access, scraping, and machine-learning use) applies to
every briefing and to the catalogue itself**, including the prohibition on bulk
retrieval and on using briefings to train, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark, or
ground any model. **Section 2 (named-seat)** applies: unlocking a briefing on your
seat and circulating it inside your firm is a breach, and firm-wide access
requires a team licence.

Permitted quotation of a briefing is governed by **Section 6 (Permitted reuse and
required attribution)** of the Master Terms, including the requirement to carry the
storefront source line, institutional attribution to Faraday Intelligence, a live
hyperlink, and the vintage.

## BL-6. Nothing here is advice

**Section 7 (No advice and no reliance), Section 8 (Scores, forecasts, and
vintages), Section 9 (Third-party data pass-through), and Section 10 (AI-assisted
content) of the Master Terms apply in full to every briefing.** Briefings are
Faraday's read as of a vintage, are expected to be revised, and must not be relied
on for capital allocation, siting, or transaction decisions.
